# Northwind Energy Contractor Handbook

This handbook applies to independent contractors and agency workers engaged by Northwind Energy. It does not apply to permanent employees.

## Time Off for Contractors

Contractors do not accrue paid annual leave, sick leave or parental leave from Northwind Energy. Contractors should agree planned absences with their engagement manager at least five working days in advance. Time off for contractors is unpaid.

## Timesheets

Contractors must submit weekly timesheets through the vendor portal by Monday at 12:00 for the previous week. Late timesheets are processed in the next payment cycle. Invoices are paid within 30 days of an approved timesheet.

## Equipment

Contractors receive a Northwind laptop only when their engagement exceeds three months. Personal devices may access company email only through the managed mobile application.

## Site Access

Contractor badges expire at the end of the engagement and must be returned to reception on the final day. Contractors working at solar sites must complete the site safety induction before their first visit.
