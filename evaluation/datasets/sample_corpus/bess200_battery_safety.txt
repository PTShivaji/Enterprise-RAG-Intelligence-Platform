# BESS-200 Battery Energy Storage System Safety Guide

The BESS-200 is a 200 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery storage cabinet installed alongside Northwind solar sites.

## Thermal Runaway

Thermal runaway is a self-sustaining temperature increase in a battery cell. If a cell temperature above 60 degrees Celsius is reported, the battery management system disconnects the affected module automatically. Never open a cabinet that is emitting smoke; evacuate to a distance of at least 30 metres and call the emergency services.

## Inspection Schedule

Battery cabinets must be visually inspected every month. The fire suppression system must be inspected and certified every six months by an accredited contractor. Battery modules must be capacity-tested once per year.

## Firmware

BESS-200 firmware updates are distributed by the battery management vendor and must be installed by a certified technician using the vendor service tool. Firmware must never be updated while the system is charging.

## Personal Protective Equipment

Technicians must wear insulated gloves rated to 1000 volts, safety glasses and arc-flash rated clothing when working inside a battery cabinet.
