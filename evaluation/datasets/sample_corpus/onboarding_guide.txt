# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Northwind Energy. This guide describes your first weeks with the company.

## Before Your First Day

You will receive your laptop by courier three days before your start date. Your manager will send you a first-week schedule.

## First Week

On your first day you will meet your onboarding buddy, an experienced colleague who supports you during your first three months. You must complete the mandatory security awareness training within your first five working days.

## Probation Period

All new employees complete a six month probation period. A probation review meeting with your manager takes place at the end of month three and again at the end of month six.

## Benefits Enrollment

You must enrol in the health insurance plan within 30 days of your start date. The company pension scheme matches employee contributions up to 6 percent of base salary.
