# Information Security Policy

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who access Northwind Energy information systems.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are changed only when there is evidence or suspicion of compromise; routine periodic password rotation is not required. Passwords must never be shared, including with IT support staff.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote access, for email and for all administrator accounts. Approved authentication methods are the company authenticator app and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not an approved method.

## Device Security

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption with BitLocker. Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the service desk within 1 hour.

## Data Classification

Information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment data and employee medical records, may only be stored on encrypted company systems and must never be sent by email.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents, including phishing emails, must be reported to the security team at security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate an incident yourself.
